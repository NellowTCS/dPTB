# Basic story overview

## core concept

dPTB is a horror visual novel set within a recovered desktop environment.

Players explore a browser-like (aka it's like ChromeOS) operating system known seemingly as d̷PTB 
They explore logs, messages, applications, dev docs, archives, journal entries, etc left behind by its creator and previous users

The project seems abandoned, half-finished

it isn't empty

## themes?

- identity, digital preservation, loneliness, creation, memory, fear of being forgotten, some yandere, some shuudere

## mc: Ada

she was the original creator of d̷PTB
she was an independent developer

d̷PTB was a passion project that she poured everything she had into it

(is that literal? who knows)

By the time our player arrives, it's kinda blurred

who or what is ada 
is she the software
archive?
previous users

## important stuff

the player is *never* given a complete explanation
ever

like
i want stuff to remain incomplete

(also that makes writing the story easier /hj)

## d̷PTB

no one ever realizes or learns (not even me) what it stands for

there's a bunch of possibilities

- Don't Press the Button
- Do Press the Button
- Desktop Platform in the Browser
- Distributed Personality Transfer Buffer

and many more haha

There is **no** canonical answer

## le button

the button was originally kinda insignificant

test control, debug widget, forgotten interface element, who knows

It was *never* intended to be important

Players made it important
The story begins because the player interacts with it.

## previous users

many users have explored d̷PTB before

they leave behind traces

logs, messages, documents, theories, warnings

et cetera

not all of it agrees, not all of them can be trusted...

could it be that ada is modifying them?
could it be something *else* is?

could it be ada remembers wrong?
ada changes herself?

...

## the archive

d̷PTB contains hundreds maybe thousands of files

the archive seems to preserve information.

Over time the player begins to suspect it's doing more than preserving.

The archive may be reconstructing someone.

## the central question, aka what the story is about

the mystery part isn't that "can Ada escape?"

the mystery is...

"Who or what is Ada?"

we do find out
